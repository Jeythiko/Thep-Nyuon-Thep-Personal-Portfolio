A Brief Story About My Educational Journey

Duel gɔrkä dä tuɔɔkä jɛ mee ta̱a̱ kɛ run bärɔw(7), rɛy wec mi tɔt mi coal kɛ i Akobo, rɛy wec kä Jinup Thodan. It was a time when attending school for me was just like going to play ground.

Then my dad had to keep pushing me to go to school. Being a schoolboy was never part of my childhood dreams because, at that young age, I didn't understand what education truly meant or how important it would become in my life.

Gradually, I began to embrace education and make it part of my life. Every day, I walked to school with a growing determination to learn, improve, and build a better future, even when I faced challenges that could have held me back.

For this journey, I give my deepest gratitude to Williams Nyuon, my beloved father, whose persistence and encouragement never allowed me to give up, and above all, to Almighty God, whose grace and guidance have brought me this far.

Today, I study Computer Science at Gambella University, where I continue chasing my dreams, developing technical knowledge, computing experience and problem-solving skills.

I enjoy learning how technology works, writing code, understanding computer networks, and exploring ways to use technology to solve real-world problems.

Beyond technology, I value faith, responsibility, self-discipline, family, and continuous growth. I believe that becoming a better person requires learning from mistakes and taking responsibility for the future.
